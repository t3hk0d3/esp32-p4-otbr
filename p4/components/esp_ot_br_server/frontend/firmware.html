Firmware

The board runs two pieces of firmware: the border router on the ESP32-P4 and the radio on the ESP32-C6. Install both from the same release.

New network settings are on trial. The board goes back to the previous settings in - unless you keep them from the management interface.

Radio recovery

-

If the radio does not start even after a radio firmware update, fit the maintenance jumper and reload this page. The board can then rewrite the C6 from scratch and show its console. How to fit the jumper

Recover rewrites the C6 base image through its ROM loader and restarts the radio. Afterwards install the radio firmware again. Remove the jumper when you are done.
