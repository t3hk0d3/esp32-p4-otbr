Thread Commissioner

The commissioner authenticates and authorises new Thread devices (joiners) onto the network using a pre-shared key for the device (PSKd).

6 to 32 digits and capital letters (no I, O, Q or Z), as printed on the device or its QR code. Then start joining on the device.
