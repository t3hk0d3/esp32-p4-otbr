What Become leader does
  1. This board starts a new partition with itself as leader. It is the same Thread network (same name, key, channel and devices); only the partition is new.
  2. The other routers move over. They see a partition with a higher leader weight and join it, the current leader (the current leader) included, which becomes a normal router. Their end devices follow them.
  3. Traffic pauses briefly. While routers rejoin, mesh traffic can drop for a few seconds. Devices keep their addresses, and nothing needs to be paired again.
  4. Nothing else changes. The network settings (dataset) stay the same, and the current leader keeps working as a border router.

Afterwards: the role is not saved, only the weight is. If this board restarts or is offline for more than about 2 minutes, another router takes over and keeps the role, even with a lower weight. Press Become leader again then. The higher weight only decides who wins when partitions merge, for example after the leader is lost.

Why it can be greyed out: OpenThread only lets a router take over from a leader with a strictly lower weight, so a misconfigured board can't steal the role back and forth.
