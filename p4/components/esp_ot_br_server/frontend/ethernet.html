What happens when you apply

  1. The board saves the new settings as a trial and reboots. This page loses its connection.
  2. Open the device page again at -.
  3. Press Keep new settings within 5 minutes. If you don't, or the board can't be reached, it goes back to the current settings by itself.

Locked out? Wait 5 minutes for the revert, or run net reset on the USB console to go back to a single untagged port with DHCP.
