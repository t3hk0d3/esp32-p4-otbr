Radios

Every radio the board knows. Each one runs this board's Thread border router, serves its serial line to another computer, or does nothing.

Thread runs on: ESP32-C6
RadioFirmwareModeStateActivity

Unplugged radios stay listed with their settings and get them back when plugged in again. Every plugged-in stick is checked first (about 3 s); a mode its firmware can't run stays off.
