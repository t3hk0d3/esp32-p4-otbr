# P4 case v6: implementation plan

Status: approved 2026-11-12 and implemented. Branch `feat/case` (PR #4, rebased on master 6db33e2); work continues in that PR.
Sketches: `docs/images/case/v6-proposals.svg`, `v6-ear-side.svg` and `v6-ears.svg` (generated by `case/sketches/sketch_v6.py`, to
scale, with board parts taken from the vendor model).

## Context

The PR #4 parts were printed. What's validated and stays as it is: the corner-tab bumps (BUMP_H 0.4, 45° hold face,
0.1 mm preload), the keyhole necks, and the standoff blocks with their Ø2.2 pilots. v6 changes:

1. **Taller case, 3 mm lid:** the walls and the lid top go up 1 mm (case Z 27.6 → 28.6). The rebate stays 1 mm deep
   (floor 26.6 → 27.6) and the lid's underside stays at 25.6, so nothing inside moves. The lid is 3 mm thick inside
   the walls (it flexed at 2 mm).
2. **Side screw ears, two options built side by side** (`ears="outside"` or `"inside"`), so both can be printed as
   full cases and compared. Outside is the default (`docs/models/`, previews). The other option is written alongside
   as `*_inside-ears.stl`. One per side wall, at Y 32, in both options.

   **Option O, outside ear:**
   - **Wall top:** over a 10.4 mm stretch, the wall drops to the lid's underside (case Z 25.6) across its full
     thickness. The lid fills that gap at its full 3 mm and runs out flush with the outer face.
   - **Ear:** hangs from that edge into a pocket in the wall's outer face. It's 10 mm wide and 1.6 mm thick, flush with
     the face, with a half-circle bottom of R5 centred on the screw (lowest point case Z 17.3). The pocket follows it
     with a 0.2 mm gap.
   - **Screw:** the ear has a Ø2.9 clearance hole with a 90° countersink (Ø5.0) on the screw axis (case Z 22.3). The
     wall behind it has a Ø2.2 pilot (as the standoffs) through the 2.4 mm left behind the pocket.
   - **Screws to use:** M2.5 × 6 flat head (flush), or the board's M2.5 × 6 pan head (sits about 1.7 mm proud and bears
     on the countersink's rim). Either way the tip pokes about 2 mm into the case, so full thread spans the wall; the
     nearest board parts are about 6 mm lower.
   - **No bumps on the ears:** the corner tabs keep doing the clicking. Tightening clamps the ear against the wall
     (compression only), and the self-tapping thread goes into the base, so the ear itself never sees thread forces.
   - **Board fit:** nothing new is inside the cavity, so fitting the board is untouched.

   **Option I, inside ear with a buttress:**
   - **Ear:** a block hanging from the lid's underside, 12 mm along the wall, 5 mm thick, down to case Z 18.5, 0.2 mm
     off the wall.
   - **Buttress:** a 45° buttress runs from the ear's bottom inner edge up to the lid's underside, about 7.1 mm
     deeper. 45° gussets (3 mm) at both ends sit under the lid, across the ear's thickness.
   - **Grille:** cells whose outline overlaps an ear's footprint on the lid's underside are left out.
   - **Screw:** a Ø3.0 clearance hole through the 4 mm wall at case Z 22.0, with a 90° countersink (Ø5.0) at the outer
     face. A Ø2.4 blind pilot in the ear stops 0.4 mm short of its inner face: about 4.6 mm of thread. M2.5 × 10 flat
     head.
   - **Board fit:** the ears hang from the lid, which goes on after the board, so fitting the board is untouched. Board
     parts under the ear and buttress reach case Z 14.3 on the left; on the right, the capacitors reach 16.4.
3. **Break-off curtain for the 40-pin slot:**
   - Changed while implementing (your choice): the header-end wall is 3 mm, so the case is 76 mm long. In the 2 mm
     wall a centred groove left 0.5 mm lips, and the tongue couldn't reach 0.8 mm. Now the groove is 1.2 mm wide with
     0.9 mm walls, the tongue is 0.8 mm (0.2 mm clear each side) and the curtain is 2.6 mm thick; the numbers below
     are the original ones.
   - The slot's floor drops from case Z 22.1 to 20.6 (5.0 mm clear under the lid's underside). It still runs the
     full inner width.
   - The floor gets a groove 1.0 mm wide and 0.8 mm deep, centred in the wall.
   - The lid fills the slot, flush with the wall's outer face. Above case Z 25.6 the fill is part of the lid.
   - Below that hangs a 1.6 mm curtain in 4 segments (about 13.6 mm, 0.4 mm slits). Each has a tongue (0.7 × 0.6 mm)
     sitting in the groove, and stops 0.2 mm above the floor.
   - The break line is at case Z 25.6, along a printed layer: V-notches on both faces leave 0.8 mm.
4. **microSD:**
   - The 0.4 mm lip over the slot goes.
   - Above the slot only, the cable wall is thickened 1 mm inward, across the scoop's width plus 1 mm each side. Its
     underside is a 1 mm horizontal ledge at the slot's top.
   - The scoop becomes 2.2 mm deep at the slot's top edge, 10 mm wide (X 40.5–50.5) and 3 mm tall, leaving 0.8 mm
     (two lines) behind it.
5. **SMA hole:** back to a round top with the flat at the bottom (no teardrop). The print showed the round top needs
   no support, so the droop allowance for small round tops rises from 0.5 to 1.0 mm (spans ≤ 8 mm).

## Styleguide

`STYLEGUIDE.md`, section "Case model" (with ruff at line length 88, as `make lint` uses it):

- every dimension is a named constant in `p4_case/params.py`;
- geometry builders are pure `build_*() -> Part`;
- checks return `CheckResult` and never raise for a failure;
- tests are a class per scenario with the docstring `"<module>.<function>: <scenario>"` and one `test_<effect>` per
  effect;
- the print STLs in `docs/models/` are refreshed by `make case`, and a test fails while they're stale.

## Files

- **`case/p4_case/params.py`:**
  - `TOP`, `REBATE_FLOOR`;
  - `EAR_STYLE = "outside"` (the default option);
  - new `EAR_*` (outside: Y, width, thickness, screw Z, clearance and countersink sizes, wall pilot) and `IN_EAR_*`
    (inside: length, thickness, bottom, buttress, gussets, screw Z, pilot, wall clearance), `CURTAIN_*`,
    `SLOT_GROOVE_*` and `SD_WALL_IN` constants;
  - `HEADER_SLOT_BOTTOM` (from `HEADER_SLOT_CLEAR` 5.0);
  - the `SCOOP_*` constants (`SCOOP_LIP` goes).
- **`case/p4_case/base.py`:** `build_base(ears: str = P.EAR_STYLE)`.
  - The shell (new height), `_header_slot` (deeper, groove), `_cable_wall_openings` (ledge, scoop) and `_sma`
    (teardrop removed) change.
  - New `_ear_pockets` (outside: wall-top cut, U-shaped pocket, wall pilot) and `_ear_wall_holes` (inside: clearance
    hole, countersink).
- **`case/p4_case/lid.py`:**
  - `_plate` (3 mm);
  - new `_outside_ears` (lid fill over the wall cut, U-shaped ear, clearance hole, countersink) and `_inside_ears`
    (block, buttress, end gussets, blind pilot; no grille cells over them);
  - new `_curtain` (fill flush with the outer face, segments, notches, tongues);
  - `build_lid(curtain: bool = True, ears: str = P.EAR_STYLE)`: with `curtain=False`, the lid as if all four
    segments were broken out.
- **`case/p4_case/checks.py`:**
  - `opening_paths`: the header-slot probe runs against the lid with its curtain broken out.
  - `opening_size`: the closed lid must leave no header-slot passage; the broken-out passage is reported, not limited.
  - `lid_clearance`: the ribbon path is checked against the broken-out lid.
  - new `ear_screws(base, lid, ears)`: each ear's screw path (wall and ear) is coaxial. The thread length is at
    least 2.2 mm in the wall (outside) or 4.5 mm in the ear (inside).
  - `run_all` takes `ears`.
  - `DROOP_MAX` 1.0.
- **`case/p4_case/build.py`:** builds and checks both options. It writes `p4_case_{base,lid}.{stl,step}` (default) and
  `p4_case_{base,lid}_inside-ears.{stl,step}`, and prints each option's check results.
- **`case/sketches/sketch_v6.py`:** done (throwaway). `coupons_v6.py` is dropped: the full cases are printed instead.
- **Tests:** `case/tests/test_base.py`, `test_lid.py`, `test_checks.py`.
- **Docs:**
  - `docs/case.md`:
    - the two ear options and their files;
    - parts: 2 × M2.5 × 6 flat head (or the board's pan heads) for outside, 2 × M2.5 × 10 flat head for inside;
    - assembly: screw the ears after the lid clicks in, snug only; breaking out curtain segments;
    - the tuning table (`EAR_PILOT_D`, `IN_EAR_PILOT_D`, `CURTAIN_NECK`);
  - the spec;
  - `docs/models/` and the preview PNGs, refreshed by `make case`.

## BDD skeleton

```
p4_case.base.build_base
    On default parameters (v6)
        - is 28.6 mm tall overall, with the rebate floor at case Z 27.6
        - cuts the header slot the full inner width from case Z 20.6 up through the wall top
        - cuts a 1.0 × 0.8 mm groove along the slot floor, centred in the wall
        - drops each side wall's top to case Z 25.6 across its full thickness over Y 26.8–37.2
        - cuts a 1.6 mm pocket into each side wall's outer face: 10.4 mm wide, half-circle bottom R5.2 about (Y 32, Z 22.3)
        - drills a Ø2.2 pilot from each pocket's back face through the wall on the screw axis (2.4 mm of wall)
        - leaves no lip between the microSD slot and the scoop
        - cuts the scoop 2.2 mm deep at the slot's top edge, X 40.5–50.5, 3 mm tall
        - keeps 0.8 mm of wall behind the scoop's deepest point
        - thickens the cable wall 1 mm inward above the microSD slot only (not beside it, not below it)
        - gives the SMA hole a round top (no teardrop) and keeps the flat at the bottom
        - keeps the corner-tab dents, keyhole necks and standoff blocks unchanged (existing tests stay green)
    With ears="inside"
        - has a Ø3.0 clearance hole through each side wall at (Y 32, case Z 22.0)
        - countersinks it 90° to Ø5.0 at the outer face
        - has no wall-top cut and no pocket

p4_case.lid.build_lid
    On default parameters (curtain on)
        - has its top at case Z 28.6 and its underside at 25.6 (3 mm thick inside the walls)
        - fills each wall-top cut at its full 3 mm, flush with the outer face, 0.2 mm clear of the cut's ends
        - has one ear per side wall: 10 mm wide, 1.6 mm thick, flush with the outer face, half-circle bottom R5
        - has a Ø2.9 clearance hole and a 90° countersink to Ø5.0 in each ear, on the wall pilot's axis
        - has no bump on the ears
        - fills the header slot above case Z 25.6 flush with the wall's outer face
        - hangs four 1.6 mm curtain segments with 0.4 mm slits between them below case Z 25.6
        - notches each segment on both faces at case Z 25.6, leaving 0.8 mm
        - puts a tongue on each segment's bottom, inside the floor groove, 0.2 mm clear of the floor
    With curtain=False
        - has no material in the header slot below case Z 25.6
        - is otherwise identical to the default lid
    With ears="inside"
        - has one block per side wall: 12 × 5 mm, from the underside down to case Z 18.5, 0.2 mm off the wall
        - has a 45° buttress from each block's bottom inner edge up to the underside
        - has 45° gussets, 3 mm, at both ends of each block
        - drills a Ø2.4 blind pilot on the wall hole's axis, stopping 0.4 mm short of the block's inner face
        - leaves out every grille cell over a block, buttress or gusset
        - has no wall-top fill and no outside ear
    Fitted on the default base
        - does not overlap the base apart from the bumps' preload

p4_case.checks.ear_screws
    On the default (outside) parts
        - passes when each ear's clearance hole is coaxial with its wall pilot and the pilot has ≥ 2.2 mm of wall
    On the inside-ear parts
        - passes when each wall hole is coaxial with its ear pilot and the pilot is ≥ 4.5 mm long
    With an ear shifted 1 mm along Y
        - fails and names the misaligned side

p4_case.checks.run_all
    For each ear option
        - runs every check on that option's base and lid; all pass

p4_case.checks.opening_paths
    On the default parts
        - passes, with the header-slot probe run against the lid with its curtain broken out
    With the curtain on
        - reports the header slot closed (no path)

p4_case.checks.opening_size
    On the default parts
        - passes: no lid cell wider than 3.5 mm and no header-slot passage while the curtain is on
        - reports the broken-out passage height (5.0 mm) in its detail

p4_case.checks.lid_clearance
    On the default parts
        - passes: lid (ears included) clears the board, the SMA envelope and the ribbon plug
        - checks the ribbon path against the lid with its curtain broken out

p4_case.checks.printability
    On the default base
        - passes with the SMA hole's round top (droop ≤ 1.0 mm over ≤ 8 mm)
    With a 20 mm ledge / a 10 × 10 mm roof (existing fixtures)
        - still fails

p4_case.checks.min_print_width
    On the default parts
        - passes: the curtain neck, the scoop's back wall and the ledge are all ≥ 0.8 mm

p4_case.build.main
    On a valid board
        - writes the default STL/STEP pair and the *_inside-ears pair
        - prints the check results for both options and exits 1 with --check if either fails
        - refreshes the STLs; the committed docs/models/ copies (default option) match a fresh build
```

## Order

1. Write the tests from the skeleton (they fail).
2. Change params and base. The base tests go green.
3. Change the lid. The lid tests go green.
4. Change the checks and the build. `make case` then runs every check green for both options; the new check brings
   the count to 30 per option.
5. Update the docs and the spec, refresh `docs/models/` and the previews, and update the PR description.
6. Commit and push to `feat/case`. The user prints and gives feedback.

## Verification

```
make case-test      # all tests
make case           # all checks pass; refreshes dist/case/ and docs/models/
make docs
uvx ruff check case/ && uvx ruff format --check case/
```

The physical check is yours:

- print both full cases (default and `*_inside-ears`);
- on each, snap the lid on, then drive both side screws, and compare how the ears hold;
- break out one curtain segment with tweezers;
- insert and remove a microSD card by the scoop;
- fit the SMA.

## Risks

- **Inside ears thread along the lid's layers**, so a self-tapping screw can split the ear between two layers. The
  buttress makes that layer about 1.7× bigger, which reduces the risk without removing it. Comparing the two full
  cases is the test; `IN_EAR_PILOT_D` is the constant to tune.
- **Outside ears: the thread is short:** 2.4 mm in the wall, about 5 turns. That's plenty for a lid, because lifting it loads the
  screws sideways, but it strips if over-tightened: snug only. The pilot is horizontal, so it lies along the base's
  layers, but it sits in a solid wall. `EAR_PILOT_D` is the constant to tune after the print.
- **The curtain's 0.8 mm neck is a guess.** If segments break off while you handle the lid, raise it; if they won't
  snap, lower it.
- **The 1 mm ledge above the microSD slot** prints as a short overhang over the cavity. The printability check treats
  it as a bridge.
